THE CATHOLIC CHURCH

The St. Anne’s Chaplaincy is a congregation of the catholic community of the Maseno University fraternity. It involves the adults who are majorly staff and people residing around Maseno, the Pontifical Missionary Children ( PMC) and the Catholic Students Association (CSA) of Maseno University. St. Anne’s Catholic Chaplaincy was elevated to a fully fledged Chaplaincy on 25th March 2023 by His Grace Most Rev. Maurice Muhatia, the archbishop of Kisumu. It now operates as a Parish under the Archdiocese of Kisumu. The current chaplain is Rev. Fr. George William Odhiambo

Catholic Students Association (CSA) Maseno University.

CSA is an association of the catholic students of Maseno university. It operates under the umbrella of the St. Annes Catholic Chaplaincy. Currently, we have a total of 6 prayer houses, 25 small Christian Communities (S.C.C) and 8 groups and movements playing different roles in the church. We all also have different year groups of students as per the years of study.
